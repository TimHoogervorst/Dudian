# Offline dictionary and curriculum

`hsk1-study-senses.json` records Dudian’s preferred dictionary sense for each headword and component character in the bundled HSK 1 list. The values are stable sense IDs from the bundled CC-CEDICT edition. These are editorial choices for Library practice.

The bundled CC-CEDICT 1.0 archive is the unmodified MDBG export dated 2026-11-23T09:02:41Z (125,463 entries). Credit goes to the CC-CEDICT community, the MDBG editors, and Paul Andrew Denisowski, creator of CEDICT. The archive includes the publisher’s original notices. The six curriculum lists are derived from Complete HSK Vocabulary by Yanis Zafirópulos, commit `7ac65bf1a6387d35f1ade478906172a19311c7f9`. The bundled lists retain simplified headwords grouped by their earliest six-level HSK 2.0 assignment. The source combines certain written forms, yielding 4,991 distinct headwords. Three entries absent from the dictionary export retain the upstream readings and glosses in the fallback dataset. The upstream dictionary-derived glosses are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
