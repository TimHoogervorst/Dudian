# Milestone tests

The dashboard and Progression page show the same milestone test. Whole HSK levels 1 through 6 each have a test. Fractional thresholds continue to unlock reading formats during ordinary practice.

## Progress and eligibility

The progress bar stops at the earliest whole level reached that the learner still needs to pass. Existing learners start with their earliest unpassed level. Once a learner reaches a score threshold, a later drop preserves that achievement. Passing a test is permanent. After a pass, the bar shows the current underlying score up to the next unpassed level.

Reaching the next whole HSK score unlocks that test and starts preparation, even if some characters are still unseen. Ordinary reading continues to favor unseen characters from the next unpassed level while preserving review slots.

The dashboard links to the reached milestone on Progression. Selecting a whole level on the Progression scale shows its locked, required, or passed test, along with any saved results for that level. During preparation the meter counts completed reading passages, including their questions. Passed levels offer practice retakes in the same panel.

## Test content

Each test has ten scored sections. Two books, two dialogues, and one article each have two comprehension questions. A word-practice section follows each reading and contains five vocabulary exercises (25 in total), mixing English-meaning choices with Chinese-to-English matching by tap or drag.

Sample from the full vocabulary pool for the designated level. Each passage uses the ordinary Hard, Short reading generator with an open subject and one chapter. The exam level sets the vocabulary and character ceiling for the entire attempt, including retakes. Earlier levels may supply background vocabulary. Speaker names and displayed examples also follow the character ceiling. Test formats use only unlocks strictly below the tested level. HSK 1 uses children’s books, letters, and two-person chats. Recipes and three-person chats become eligible on the HSK 2 test. Book passages follow the chapter word budget, articles use the document’s paragraph roles, and dialogues use the Hard message count. Scored questions focus on the designated level. Learners can revisit every section. The test follows the reader’s pinyin preference. Each passage paragraph has a speaker button, and each question has one that reads the prompt and its choices. Dictionary lookup and translation controls are hidden throughout the test.

## Taking a test

The section sidebar includes a final Submit test entry. Draft selections and the current section survive navigation and refresh. Learners can change any answer until final submission. If answers are blank, the submission page gives their count, links to the first one, and lets the learner submit anyway.

Grade once at hand-in. A blank answer fails the test. With every item answered, up to three reading comprehension mistakes and up to three word-practice mistakes pass. Four mistakes in either category fail the attempt. Each incorrect matching pair counts as one word-practice mistake. The result summary lists incorrect and unanswered items with the learner’s choice and correct answer. Done discards the attempt. The earned level survives dismissal.

## Preparation and retakes

Eligible levels prepare a fresh test in the background. The dashboard shows the next required milestone. Progression also offers an independent practice retake for each passed level. Start or resume a prepared test, retry a failed preparation, or delete the current attempt on Progression. Submitting or deleting an attempt starts preparing its replacement. All attempts leave reading strength, encounter dates, and review schedules unchanged.
