# Milestone tests

The dashboard and Progression page show the same milestone test. Whole HSK levels 1 through 6 each have a test. Fractional thresholds continue to unlock reading formats during ordinary practice.

## Progress and eligibility

The progress bar stops at the earliest whole level reached that the learner still needs to pass. Existing learners start with their earliest unpassed level. Once a learner reaches a score threshold, a later drop preserves that achievement. Passing a test is permanent. After a pass, the bar shows the current underlying score up to the next unpassed level.

Generating a level’s test requires an encounter with every character assigned to that level. A completed passage, a word lookup, or a submitted ordinary practice answer can each credit the characters involved. An incorrect answer still counts as exposure. Previewing vocabulary earns no credit. Honor existing encounter records, including those reconstructed from reading strength. Once earned, character coverage survives a manual strength reset.

When ordinary reading introduces new characters, prefer unseen ones from the next unpassed level while preserving review slots for due or weak items. Once the score reaches the threshold, the dashboard and Progression page offer a targeted Library practice session for any missing characters, labeled with the remaining count. Explicit Library selections retain their chosen scope. After the final character encounter, offer Generate test even if the underlying score has dipped below the threshold.

## Test content

For the first release, each level uses 15 sections. Nine contain fresh reading passages, with three each of Book, Article, and Dialogue. Each passage has two scored comprehension questions. The other six sections contain ten vocabulary exercises in total, mixing English-meaning choices with Chinese-to-English matching by tap or drag. The Introduction lists the sections and question and exercise counts.

Sample from the full vocabulary pool for the designated level. Passages at higher levels can also use earlier vocabulary. Scored questions focus on the designated level. Learners can reread the current passage and use pinyin and Mandarin audio. Hide English glosses in the Introduction, word meaning previews, dictionary lookups, and translations during the test.

## Taking a test

The dashboard and Progression page offer Generate test, Start test, or Continue test according to the attempt's state. Save answers, current section, mistake count, and outcome across visits. Keep the assessment separate from character and word strength, last-seen dates, and review schedules.

Learners can change selections until they submit. Submission locks those answers and shows the updated mistake count. Each wrong comprehension answer, meaning choice, or matched pair costs one mistake. Completing all sections with up to three mistakes earns a pass. The fourth mistake ends the attempt. After either outcome the learner can review submitted answers and explanations. After a failure, generate a fresh test to try again.
